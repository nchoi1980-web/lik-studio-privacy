Privacy Policy — Still Blocks

Developer / Publisher: LIK Studio
Effective date: October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Still Blocks handles information when you use the application.

1. Information you provide directly

Still Blocks does not require users to create an account and does not ask users to provide a name, email address, phone number, postal address, payment card information, photos, audio recordings, messages, precise location, or date of birth.

2. Age category and child/teen advertising protections

Still Blocks asks the user to select one of three age categories: Under 13, Age 13–17, or Age 18+. The selected age category is stored locally on the device and is used only to configure the application's advertising behavior. The app does not request or store the user's exact date of birth.

The selected age category is not sent to LIK Studio as a user profile. Before an age category is selected, the app does not initialize or request advertising. For users Under 13, the app configures Google Mobile Ads for child-directed treatment, limits ad content to a G rating, and disables personalized advertising. For users Age 13–17, the app uses Google's teen treatment and disables personalized advertising. For users Age 18+, standard Google Mobile Ads settings and the applicable consent flow may be used.

3. Information processed automatically

Still Blocks uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK to display advertising. Depending on the selected age category, applicable law, consent status, and Google Mobile Ads configuration, the SDK may automatically collect and share technical and usage information, including:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate a device's general location;
  • user product interactions, such as app launches, taps, and video or ad views;
  • diagnostic and performance information, such as app launch time, hang rate, and energy usage;
  • device or account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and App Set ID where available and permitted by the applicable advertising configuration.

According to Google's documentation, this information may be used for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and compliance purposes. Data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS.

4. Local app data

Game progress, preferences, settings, and the selected age category may be stored locally on the user's device so the application can preserve gameplay state and apply the appropriate advertising configuration between sessions. LIK Studio does not operate a separate user account system or cloud database for this application.

5. Third-party service

The application uses Google AdMob. Google's handling of information is governed by Google's own policies. Google's Privacy Policy is available at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Data sharing

LIK Studio does not sell personal information. Technical and usage information described above may be collected or shared automatically with Google through the Google Mobile Ads SDK for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and related service purposes, subject to the age-based advertising configuration described above.

7. Data retention and deletion

LIK Studio does not maintain user accounts or a separate server-side user database for Still Blocks. Local game data, preferences, and the selected age category can generally be removed by clearing the application's storage or uninstalling the application. Information processed by Google is retained and deleted according to Google's applicable policies and user controls.

8. Security

Where data is transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK, Google states that it is encrypted in transit using TLS. Still Blocks does not ask users to submit passwords or payment card information to LIK Studio.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the application's features, third-party SDKs, or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.

10. Privacy contact

Privacy questions or requests concerning Still Blocks can be submitted to LIK Studio through the public privacy inquiry page.
