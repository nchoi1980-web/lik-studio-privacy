Privacy Policy — Still Blocks

Developer / Publisher: LIK Studio
Effective date: October 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Still Blocks handles information when you use the application.

1. Information you provide directly

Still Blocks does not require users to create an account and does not ask users to provide a name, email address, phone number, postal address, payment card information, photos, audio recordings, messages, or precise location.

2. Information processed automatically

Still Blocks uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK to display advertising. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share technical and usage information, including:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate a device's general location;
  • user product interactions, such as app launches, taps, and video or ad views;
  • diagnostic and performance information, such as app launch time, hang rate, and energy usage;
  • device or account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and App Set ID where available.

According to Google's documentation, this information may be used for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and compliance purposes. Data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS.

3. Local app data

Game progress, preferences, and settings may be stored locally on the user's device so the application can preserve gameplay state between sessions. LIK Studio does not operate a separate user account system or cloud database for this application.

4. Third-party service

The application uses Google AdMob. Google's handling of information is governed by Google's own policies. Google's Privacy Policy is available at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Data sharing

LIK Studio does not sell personal information. The technical and usage information described above may be collected or shared automatically with Google through the Google Mobile Ads SDK for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and related service purposes.

6. Data retention and deletion

LIK Studio does not maintain user accounts or a separate server-side user database for Still Blocks. Local game data can generally be removed by clearing the application's storage or uninstalling the application. Information processed by Google is retained and deleted according to Google's applicable policies and user controls.

7. Security

Where data is transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK, Google states that it is encrypted in transit using TLS. Still Blocks does not ask users to submit passwords or payment card information to LIK Studio.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the application's features, third-party SDKs, or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.

9. Privacy contact

Privacy questions or requests concerning Still Blocks can be submitted to LIK Studio through the public privacy inquiry page.
